# Glossary

## Athlete

A sportsperson whose **sporting nationality** with their international federation is Belgium, in a sport on the Olympic programme (summer or winter), who has at least one **senior international performance**. They become an Athlete from that first performance. Youth, junior and U23 athletes are not Athletes until they compete at senior international level. Paralympic athletes are not Athletes in this domain. An athlete who changes sporting nationality keeps their Belgian history but leaves the active roster.

## Senior international performance

Competing in a senior event at international level: a senior Championship, a senior World Cup or circuit event, or the Olympic Games. These do not count: youth, junior and U23 events (including their Championships, the Youth Olympic Games and EYOF), smaller events sanctioned by an international federation, and national championships. The Olympic Games always count, including the men's football tournament despite its age limit.

## Olympic cycle

The period leading up to one Olympic Games, ending with that Games. Two cycles run side by side: the summer cycle (currently LA 2028) and the winter cycle (currently French Alps 2030).

## Championship

A title event in an Olympic sport at continental level (Europeans) or world level (Worlds), as opposed to the Olympic Games themselves or series and circuit events. This includes the FIFA World Cup and UEFA Euros in football, and every division of the IIHF World Championship in ice hockey. Where a sport holds separate title events by venue or format (indoor and outdoor, short course and long course), each is its own Championship, as are the Europeans and the Worlds.

## Expectation

A forecast of an **Entry**'s outcome at a competition, recorded before it takes place. It is either **external** (world ranking, seeding, published projections, Belgian Olympic Committee targets) or **personal** (the user's own pick). It is compared afterwards with the **outcome**.

## Participation

Belgium taking part in a competition with at least one **Entry**. It is **confirmed** once an entry list, start list, **Selection** or result names a Belgian Entry, and **likely** when a Belgian Entry took part in the previous edition or is in contention, or when the user adds it by hand. A competition appears in the calendar only with a confirmed or likely Participation; Championships and the Olympic Games always appear.
_Avoid_: expected (that is an **Expectation**)

## Entry

Whoever competes for Belgium in one event at one competition: either an **Athlete** or a **Team**. Results, Expectations and quota places attach to an Entry.

## Team

A unit of two or more Athletes competing together for Belgium in one event, with its own lasting identity and a **roster** for each competition. By default there is one Team for Belgium in an event, and it keeps its identity as its roster changes: the Red Lions, a 4×400 m relay, a gymnastics team. Where Belgium can field several at once (beach volleyball pairs, doubles, multi-person boats), each standing partnership is its own Team, and a new partnership is a new Team. Every multi-athlete entry is a Team; there are no crews.

## Roster

The Athletes credited on a Team's entry at one competition, as the official result or squad list names them. That includes heats-only relay members where the federation credits them, and excludes reserves who are not named. Everyone on the roster shares the Team's result in their own Athlete record.

## Phase

One of the five stages that every **Championship** and Olympic Games passes through, in order: **Standards**, **Qualification**, **Selection**, **Live** and **After**. Championships and Games use the same phases. The Games can also be viewed one sport at a time.

- **Standards:** from the start of the cycle until the qualification system or entry standards are published. Nothing is shown before publication except the expected publication date, if known. Once published, the standards remain visible as what each **Qualification track** needs.
- **Qualification:** each potential Entry's current qualification status and its **Qualification tracks**.
- **Selection:** the final list of Entries, as named by the selector (the national federation for a Championship, the Belgian Olympic Committee for the Games) and confirmed by the international federation's entry list. Personal **Expectations** are entered here.
- **Live:** what Belgian Entries do day by day, plus the medal table.
- **After:** Expectations compared with outcomes, and Belgium's best results. Expectations are compared only here, never on an Athlete's own record.

## Qualification track

One route or requirement an Entry has to meet to compete at a Championship or Games: an entry standard, a world-ranking position, a quota place, a federation or Belgian Olympic Committee selection, and so on. Each track records what is needed, what the Entry has so far, and whether it is met. An Athlete's record tracks qualification for two targets: the next Olympic Games in their **Olympic cycle**, shown throughout the cycle, and their next Championship, shown only for the one coming up next.

## Quota place

One place for Belgium in one event at one Olympic Games, as allocated by the international federation under the IOC qualification system. It is either **NOC-owned** (Belgium decides who fills it) or **named** to the Entry that earned it. It moves from **Provisional** (held on today's list or ranking) to **Allocated** (confirmed by the federation) to **Accepted** (Belgium confirms it will use it) to **Entered** (filled by a selected Entry at the entries deadline), or ends as **Declined** or **Lost**. Championships have no quota places, only per-nation entry caps.
_Avoid_: slot, spot, ticket

## Qualification status

Where one Entry stands for one target (the next Olympic Games or the next Championship): **Not in contention**, **At risk** (outside the places if qualifying closed today), **On track** (inside them), **Eligible** (meets the federation's criteria but competes with other Belgians for an NOC-owned place), **Qualified** (holds a named place, or is the only Belgian candidate for an NOC-owned one), **Selected**, **Not selected** or **Withdrawn**. It belongs to the Athlete or Team, never to individual roster members. Every status records its source and the date it was last checked.
_Avoid_: nominated (no separate nomination step)

## Selection

The selector's final naming of Entries for a competition: the national federation for a Championship, the Belgian Olympic Committee board (on the federation's proposal) for the Games.
_Avoid_: nomination

## Active

An **Athlete** or **Team** that is currently followed: shown in the default list, given **Qualification tracks** and a **Qualification status**, and counted towards a likely **Participation**. It is active while its latest senior international performance is less than 12 months old, in every sport, or while a **Hold** is in place. Only appearing in the official result counts (DNF and DSQ included); DNS, entry or start lists, unnamed reserves and upcoming Selections do not. It becomes **inactive** at once on retirement (for a Team, when the partnership dissolves) or on a change of sporting nationality. Inactive Athletes and Teams keep their full record and can still be found. A new performance makes them active again, clearing a retirement or dissolution, except after a change of sporting nationality. A Team's status is its own, independent of its roster members.
_Avoid_: archived

## Hold

A curated keep-active-until date, with a reason, that keeps an **Athlete** or **Team** **Active** through a known absence such as injury, pregnancy or a sabbatical. After that date the 12-month rule applies again.
